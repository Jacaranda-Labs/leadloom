What was built

Three things most lead forms cannot do

Anyone can put a checkbox on a page. That is not the hard part. The hard part is answering, months later, under scrutiny: show me that this person agreed to this specific use, on this date, under this wording.

01

Consent you can prove

The exact privacy notice is hashed and versioned at the moment of submission. The consent log is hash-chained and signed with a key the database does not hold, so a past entry cannot be quietly edited — only visibly broken.

02

Nothing sent without permission

Each destination sits behind a purpose gate. If the visitor did not agree to advertising measurement, the request to Meta is never even built. Refusal is honoured immediately, including for the contact permission your calls depend on.

03

A receipt for every lead

The visitor is told what happened to their data, destination by destination, in plain words. Non-delivery is written down too, with the purpose that blocked it — so "we did not sell this lead" becomes something you can evidence.

Why the contact consent is a separate, required box. Consent for autodialled or prerecorded calls and texts has to be a real affirmative act, given separately — it cannot be folded into "we will provide the service". A form that lets it slide is the failure pattern regulators cite, and the calls that follow are the ones that cost money. Ours refuses the submission and points at the missing box.

One page that answers "did it work, and can we prove it?"

Four tiles: what mode the system is in, how many leads have arrived, whether the consent record still verifies, and whether any lead is being sold on. Deliberately no personal data — a dashboard that shows lead PII becomes a second copy of the data you have to protect.

What is live today, and what still needs you

We would rather hand you a short list of open items than a claim that everything is finished. Both columns are true right now.

Working now

  • Form collecting on your eight verticals, with your privacy and terms links
  • Consent recorded, versioned and cryptographically verifiable
  • Purpose gate blocking every destination the visitor did not permit
  • Retention enforced at 180 days; click ids at 30 days
